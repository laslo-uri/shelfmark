# Shelfmark

**Catalog the books you actually own.**

Shelfmark is a personal bookshelf app built for real life. Point your camera at an ISBN barcode or type the number — the app looks up the title, author, and cover, and adds the book to your shelf. No account, no cloud library: your collection stays on your device.

Use it to keep track of what’s on your shelves, search your library, jot notes on individual books, and back up or move your catalog whenever you want.

Shelfmark is meant to work wherever you read — phone, desktop, or browser — so building your library feels as simple as scanning the spine of the next book you pull off the shelf.
